Publications

Resiliência Cibernética em Ambientes de TO: Uma Abordagem Baseada em Monitoramento Passivo em Conformidade com a IEC 62443

Extended Proceedings of the 26th Brazilian Symposium on Cybersecurity (SBSeg 2026) - Brazilian Computer Society (SBC) • Sep 1, 2026

Carlos W. de Souza, Gabriel Monteiro Rosa and Otávio S. M. Gomes.

The convergence of IT and OT has reduced the physical isolation of industries, exposing critical infrastructure to cyber incidents. Unlike IT, OT prioritizes availability and the physical safety of machines, equipment, the environment and people. This paper addresses vulnerabilities in industrial systems and proposes a defense-in-depth strategy based on passive monitoring and DPI. Through a practical experiment with the Modbus TCP protocol, it shows how unauthorized changes are detected and mapped to the MITRE ATT&CK for ICS framework. The results indicate that passive monitoring can contribute to IEC 62443-related security goals, especially system integrity, without introducing additional traffic into the monitored operational path.

The paper is written in Portuguese; the title is kept in the original language and the abstract above is a translation.
